Executive Summary & Vision

Restoring Autonomy Through Community Hearth

Documented on 14th August 2026, the Ghala Letu Community Pantry was structured to replace impersonal aid hand-outs with a reliable, structured communal storefront.

Our Guiding Vision

A Nairobi where no child sleeps without supper, no pupil is sent home for lacking clean uniform or books, and families preserve total dignity when navigating emergency hardship.

Scheduled Shopping Hours

Families select the exact foods, jacket sizes, and hygiene goods they need from well-organized shelving rather than receiving arbitrary pre-packed parcels.

Vetted Referral Triad

Intake pathways operate directly alongside County Children's Officers, municipal primary health clinics, and headteachers to verify genuine vulnerability.

Institutional Governance

Legal Structure & Scaled Accountability

Why our founding charter begins as a Community Based Organization (CBO) under Nairobi County statutes before transitioning toward national Public Benefit Organization (PBO) accreditation.

Phase 01 Current Phase

CBO Registration & Agility

Filing through the Nairobi County Social Development Office. Gives immediate grassroots legal entity, allows compliant local bank account operation, and sets up community leadership resolutions.

• Resolution signed by 10 Founding Neighbors • Area Sub-County Social Officer review • Dedicated dual-signatory pantry fund
verified Registered under County Social Charter
Phase 02

County Children's Officer Accords

Execution of formal Memorandums of Understanding with the Nairobi Area Advisory Council (AAC) and local administrative chiefs to protect minors and institutionalize school uniform drives.

• Child Protection Safeguarding Officer assigned • Co-referral slips with 8 partner clinics • Quarterly social auditor inspects storehouse
diversity_3 Institutional AAC Alignment
Phase 03

National PBO Act Horizon

Phased transition toward national Public Benefit Organization (PBO) standing in compliance with the Public Benefit Organisations Act (gazetted March 2026 regulations) for cross-county expansion.

• Expanded inter-county food rescue transport • KRA tax-exemption verification for food donors • Certified external GAAP transparency audit
account_balance 2026 PBO Regulatory Pathway
Founding Leadership

Accountable Civic Governance

Our founding committee consists of respected local professionals, community elders, and youth organizers bound by strict bylaws of zero conflict of interest and uncompromised volunteer service.

verified_user Mandatory Child Safeguarding Pledge

Any team member, volunteer, or donor handling child data must submit a valid Kenyan Police Certificate of Good Conduct and complete our 4-hour Safeguarding Protocol Orientation.

CP
Chairperson Civic Direction & Nairobi County Liaison

Oversees constitution adherence, convenes quarterly member assemblies, and anchors relationships with the Sub-County Social Officer.

SC
Secretary Intake Records & Safeguarding Officer

Custody of all volunteer vetting dossiers, Certificate of Good Conduct records, minute taking, and partner MOU correspondence.

TR
Treasurer Finance & Auditable Ledgers

Manages the dual-authorization CBO bank account, issues weekly reconciliation statements, and manages logistics stipends.

PL
Pantry & Closet Lead Storehouse, FIFO & Sanitation

Directs inventory inspection, clothes steaming, pantry shelving rotations, and the weekly shopping allocation shifts.
